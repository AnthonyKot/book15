# Book 15 — essay template (beats; structure.py enforces the section classes)

Kicker: `Essay N · Lem ch. N, "<Title>" (2013 tr., pp. x–y)`
H1: essay title. **Lede** (`p.lede`): the 2026 object in the reader's hand, and the 1964
sentence that described it — or failed to. ≤120 words.

1. **What Lem said** (`section.said`) — the chapter's argument in his order, ~600–900
   words, quoting verbatim with page refs. Include the 1964 baseline he extrapolated
   from. Note his own later re-grade if any.
2. **What happened** (`section.happened`) — the 2026 side, receipted; the story from
   1964 to now in ≤700 words. Every factual claim → `<!-- CHECK: id -->`.
3. **The ledger** (`table.ledger`) — 5–10 rows: `Lem (page) | 2026 (receipt) | verdict`.
   Two sub-blocks or a column: object level / problem level.
4. **Where Lem was better than us** (`section.better`) — the one thing in the chapter
   2026 discourse still hasn't caught up to. Must be specific. If none, say so and why.
5. **Where he was worst** — folded into the ledger's MISS rows plus one paragraph.
6. **Still open** (`section.open`) — ≤3 items, each with what would close it.
7. **Reading** (`div.reading`) — the sections to read in the 2013 translation, and the
   2–3 modern sources the essay leaned on.
Nav (`nav.chapter-nav`): prev / contents / next.
