### 1. 1982 “update” with hardware that did not exist (misreading Lem / Zylinska)

**Location:** “When Lem revisited the book in 1982, Zylinska reports, he updated the chapter with the first commercial VR hardware — ‘virtual reality devices such as Eye Phone and Data Glove’ — as evidence the prediction was landing (p. 21). **Fix:** Date the VR examples to 1991, or to “the later essays, as Zylinska summarises them.” Say he updated *examples in the afterwords*, not the chapter.
