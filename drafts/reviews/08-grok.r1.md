### 1. Embryo screening is scored as Lem’s invisible path; the sentence that blocks that mapping is never quoted

**Location:** lede (“It will look like a dating service… the quiet one is a consumer product”); “What happened” (“The second level arrived too… at the embryo”); problem-level HIT on p. 347.

**Problem:** The section opens by naming the images we will *wrongly* expect: “Brain surgery, ‘bottled fetuses developing under the control of genetic engineering’—these are the kinds of images offered by the science fiction literature on this topic. Yet the procedures that will be used may actually be invisible” (p. 344). IVF plus embryo scoring *is* bottled fetuses under genetic assessment. He Jiankui is genetic engineering of fetuses. Both 2026 objects sit on the side Lem called the misleading picture. His invisible procedure is partner choice by a matchmaker. The essay never quotes the bottled-fetuses sentence, then treats PGT-P as the quiet path that “reads like a description of the present.”

**Fix:** Quote p. 344’s contrast in “What Lem said.” Score dating as the matchmaker (object). Score embryo work as the SF vehicle he said would *not* be the route, or as a third path. Do not let CRISPR-vs-PGT-P pay a HIT on matchmaking-vs-surgery.

### 2. The required MISS grades the wrong claim against the wrong machine, and the receipt overstates the science

**Location:** object-level MISS, p. 346 (“relatively easy… distribute widely high intelligence”); “Where he was worst”; `<!-- CHECK: karavani-2019 -->`.

**Problem:** On the page, “relatively easy” is comparative: intelligence versus “a deep transformation of man’s systemic organization” (the Dart/cannibalism argument), by means of marriage machines that “could take thousands of years” (p. 347). The row treats it as an absolute ease-claim about one-generation embryo picks. Karavani et al. 2019 give ≈2.5 IQ points *with 2019 predictors* and say the gain “will increase proportionally to the square root of the variance explained by the predictor.” Nucleus’s own page claims ~10.8 points from five embryos; Herasight (2025) claims an 8.5-point gap among three. Within-family additive variance is a real ceiling; 2.5 is not that ceiling, and “a limit no better score removes” is false of the number used to pay the MISS. Over millennial partner selection the comparative claim is not obviously dead.

**Fix:** If the negative is “one-generation embryo selection cannot move the species mean,” say so and call it the essay’s transposition, not Lem’s miss. Restore the comparison (intelligence vs systemic reorganization). Replace the frozen 2.5 with the ceiling/uptake argument, or drop the row.

**Fix:** Score the disability clause against Dor Yeshorim / PGT-M (HIT or EARLY at partner level; HIT at embryo level). Score IQ/personality separately, as he did. Cut “item for item.”
