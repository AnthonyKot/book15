### 3. The Ashby account is wrong in two places — CONFIRMED (Grok 3)

**Location:** “Generate-and-select inside the formalism is the road neither man drew”; “comprehension … is also his objection to Ashby.”

Corpus p. 261 states the disagreement precisely: Ashby begins with maximum diversity and filters it; Lem wants “significant yet not enormous diversity” produced by self-organization. Lem then explicitly describes mathematicians as a generator of formal diversity and empiricists as Ashby’s selector. Therefore formal generate-and-select is a road both men discuss, not a third road neither drew. “Comprehension” belongs to the p. 367 machine-librarian objection, not the disagreement with Ashby.

Keep FunSearch as a modern learned generator plus exact formal evaluator. Compare its source and scale of variation with the Lem/Ashby alternatives; do not claim the route was absent from their map.
