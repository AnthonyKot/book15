**2. Misreading Lem: Past premise treated as future forecast** (Severity 2)
*   **Location:** "He said technical evolution was producing 'only narrowly specialized devices'; the direction reversed."
*   **Problem:** Lem is misquoted and misread. On p. 43, Lem writes that technical evolution "has so far been moving in a kind of reverse direction to biological evolution in that it has only been creating narrowly specialized devices." He is describing the history of technology *up to 1964* as a premise, not making a forecast that it will indefinitely continue to do so. His core argument in the book is that technology must eventually achieve biological universality. The essay penalizes him for failing to predict the exact reversal he was arguing for.
*   **Fix:** Remove this as an example of a falsified forecast. Replace it with a genuine failed object-level prediction from the existing 85 rows.
