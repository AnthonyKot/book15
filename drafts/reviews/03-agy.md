1. **Severity 1 (Critical): Misreading Lem / Verdict Inflation**
- **Location:** Ledger row `"If, for economic reasons, all the civilizations were only working on reception..."` and prose `"Inside the same paragraph he identifies the asymmetry..."`
- **Problem:** The essay awards Lem a HIT for this asymmetry and praises him for stating it ("three sentences in a 1966 book"). However, in the corpus, Lem explicitly ends that exact sentence with `"...as many have already pointed out"` (p. 90). He is summarizing existing discourse, not making a novel prediction.
- **Fix:** Remove the HIT. If kept as a problem-level observation, the prose must explicitly acknowledge that Lem was summarizing the field's existing state of the art rather than inventing the dilemma.

2. **Severity 2 (High): Misreading Lem / Missing Context**
- **Location:** Ledger row and "Where he was worst": `"Radio contact 'requires some serious material investment... This kind of investment would have to be even more substantial than the current investment in research on nuclear energy'"` (p. 90).
- **Problem:** The essay creates a MISS by claiming Lem vastly overestimated the cost of listening ($10M/yr). But it omits the sentence immediately preceding the quote: `"It would require us to construct a great amount of equipment that would enable a 'search for extraterrestrials'—and possibly also transmitting stations."` Lem's budget estimate included massive interstellar transmission beacons, which *would* be astronomically expensive.
- **Fix:** Restore the omitted context (`"and possibly also transmitting stations"`) to the quote and re-evaluate. If transmission is included, his cost estimate is not a MISS.

3. **Severity 3 (High): 2026 Claims Overstatement**
- **Location:** Prose: `"Every serious attempt to leave or to listen has been paid for by individuals..."`
- **Problem:** This claim is factually false and overstated. The essay itself contradicts this by noting NASA's HRMS was a federally funded attempt. Furthermore, state-funded facilities like China's FAST telescope actively conduct SETI, and NASA recently resumed funding technosignature grants.
- **Fix:** Soften the claim to state that *dedicated* SETI facilities and the largest recent initiatives have relied on private philanthropy, rather than claiming "every serious attempt" was privately funded.
