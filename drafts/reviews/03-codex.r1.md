## 2. The cost MISS compares different quantities — CONFIRMED, but both reviewers overstate the consequence

**Reviewers:** Grok 1; Agy 2.

Corpus p. 90 says actualizing contact would require “a great amount of equipment” for searching and “possibly also transmitting stations.” It later again calls a “sizeable amount of specialized receiving equipment” extremely costly. The essay omits that context and compares HRMS/Breakthrough Listen operating commitments with annual fusion research and ITER’s cumulative construction cost.

Breakthrough Listen uses major pre-existing public facilities—including Green Bank, Parkes and MeerKAT—so its $10 million annual programme commitment is not the all-in equipment quantity Lem named. [Breakthrough’s own telescope inventory](https://breakthroughinitiatives.org/telescopes/1) confirms this arrangement.

The reviewers go too far in two respects:

- **REJECTED:** “Possibly also transmitting stations” does not prove that Lem’s comparison necessarily included a massive beacon. “Possibly” is optional, and the end of p. 90 separately insists that receiving equipment is costly.
- **REJECTED:** A $10 million search can be both real and radically incomplete. There is no logical contradiction between a cheap actual search and an OPEN verdict on the evidentiary value of its null result.

**Required revision:** restore the full p. 90 context and either assemble an all-in, like-for-like cost comparison or demote the row to OPEN. Remove “never needed to” unless independently receipted.

## 3. The private-funding thesis is false as written — CONFIRMED

**Reviewers:** Grok 2; Agy 3.

“Every serious attempt to leave or to listen has been paid for by individuals,” “the rulers never came,” and “the answer was none of them” contradict the essay’s own NASA HRMS history. They also erase public observatories and current public work:

- NASA currently funds a handful of technosignature grants. [NASA](https://science.nasa.gov/uap/faqs/)
- FAST is a state-built facility with SETI among its scientific goals. [Chinese National Astronomical Observatories](https://nao.cas.cn/news/ky/202004/t20200428_6408048.html)
- Breakthrough Listen operates on publicly owned Green Bank, Parkes and MeerKAT infrastructure.

The defensible claim is narrower: after HRMS, major dedicated US radio-SETI programmes depended heavily on philanthropy, often while using public facilities.

**Required revision:** bound the claim geographically, temporally and institutionally. Give the revised public/private claim its own marker.

## 9. The call-signs row conflates a valid argument with an already existing method — CONFIRMED in part

**Reviewer:** Grok 9.

Corpus p. 64 already describes Ozma searching for artificial regularity against galactic noise. The essay’s “now simply how the searches are built” therefore inflates the historical distance.

The p. 90 Shannon argument is nevertheless real and survives: efficient encoding can resemble noise, while discoverable beacons must expose structure. That can be graded as an argument, but at problem level and with its contemporary baseline stated.

The current receipt also merges distinct properties:

- narrow spectral bandwidth;
- simple or repetitive temporal structure;
- low-redundancy information that statistically resembles noise.

These are related but not equivalent. Modern SETI explicitly acknowledges natural spectral lines while saying that extremely narrow signals are difficult for nature to produce. [Berkeley SETI](https://seti.berkeley.edu/listen/tech.html)

**Required revision:** narrow the row to the encoding/detection argument, state that Ozma already searched for regularity, and restore the stronger claim’s scope to conversations of “supercivilizations.”

# Findings both reviewers missed

## A. Hypothesis III is gated against the wrong occurrence — CONFIRMED

The essay cites “less than twenty thousand years” to p. 82, where it describes hypothesis III’s expansion phase. `checks/quotes.tsv` registers the fragment only at p. 65, where the identical words occur in a different argument about civilization lifespan.

Because the phrase appears twice, the gate passes while failing to check the occurrence the essay actually cites. Add a p. 82 row and retain p. 65 only if that occurrence is also quoted.

## B. The claims register falsely says natural astrophysical emission is simply broadband — CONFIRMED

`narrowband-seti` says natural sources are broadband. Modern SETI documentation explicitly says nature produces both continuum emission and spectral lines; the stronger distinction is that extremely narrow signals are difficult to produce naturally. This matters because the essay’s row depends on the precise detectable feature.

## C. `starshot-2016` contains two unsupported universals — CONFIRMED

The register calls Starshot “the only funded interstellar flight programme” and the prose calls it a funded interstellar attempt. Starshot describes itself as proof-of-concept research, while NASA has separately funded interstellar-propulsion concept studies through NIAC. [NASA DEEP-IN](https://www.nasa.gov/general/deep-in-directed-energy-propulsion-for-interstellar-exploration/)

Replace “only” and distinguish a research programme from a mission.

## D. “The actual constraint was attention” is an unreceipted causal conclusion — CONFIRMED

The essay moves from spending totals to the assertion that neither money nor political will constrained SETI, only attention. Its own ATA history says funding dried up, while HRMS’s cancellation demonstrates political will. The evidence supports low priority and unstable funding; it does not isolate “attention” as the actual cause or prove that expense was irrelevant.
