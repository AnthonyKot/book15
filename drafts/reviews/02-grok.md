## 1. The “legislature” indictment is a misread of the unread half of the chapter

**Location:** “Nothing in the two-evolutions model has a place to put that”; “his frame has only one selection pressure. Everything in the chapter happens by competition in a niche.”

**Problem:** Two pages after the similarity sentence the essay treats as the thesis, Lem writes that “technical evolution is not, for the time being at least, people-free” and that “the biggest difference” is that bioevolution is “an amoral process, which is something we cannot say about technical evolution” (p. 38). He then spends the rest of the chapter on human agency: moral choice and irreversibility (pp. 47–49), states accelerating or stalling a technology by spending (p. 51), and a civilisation saying “Enough!” and refusing to automatize (p. 57). Regulation as a selector is a 2026 specification of a difference he named and developed. The essay never quotes past “Similarities.”

**Fix:** Drop or rewrite the “nowhere to put it” claim. If the political selector is the essay’s negative, score it as a problem-level row against p. 38’s “biggest difference” (he named agency; he did not model a legislature that kills the fit and saves the unfit) — not as a hole in a chapter the essay did not read.

## 2. The air-cushion MISS grades a hedged illustration as a dated forecast

**Location:** Lede: “he was rash enough to date it”; ledger row: `"further development of mechanical vehicles, which will make wheel drive obsolete"`; “Where he was worst”: “He picked the wrong successor.”

**Problem:** The governing verbs are “One can also imagine” and “it is quite likely that, say” (p. 36). He gives no year. Two sentences earlier he says cars and aeroplanes “have not yet reached their developmental peak, which is why we cannot talk about their late forms” (p. 35). The lawnmower is a worked example of the relic *pattern*, contingent on a successor that would abolish the wheel. The ledger strips the hedges; the lede invents a dating. Same class of error as scoring a reductio as a forecast (essay 4) and as the hedged entertainment application that had to be pulled from MISS to OPEN (essay 6).

**Fix:** Either keep a narrow object-level MISS on “will make wheel drive obsolete” with the hedges in the Lem cell and stop calling it dated, or move the successor to prose and let the relic row (already OPEN) carry the pattern. Do not rest “Where he was worst” on a thought-experiment.

## 3. Radio HIT is paid by a different event than the one Lem described

**Location:** Ledger: squeezed by television, radio answers with “a sudden ‘radiation of variations’… and to their appearance in new ‘ecological niches’” → AM dropped from EVs, 2023.

**Problem:** Lem’s claim is diversification under television pressure (pocket sets, hi-fi, stereophonic sound — p. 36). The receipt is one band losing a habitat to electromagnetic interference, then a lobby. That is contraction plus legislation, not radiation into niches. The AM story belongs in the legislature paragraph, not as payment for this HIT.

**Fix:** Pay the HIT with what actually continued the radiation (transistor/Walkman, FM, satellite, podcasts/streaming) or drop the row. Keep AM-in-EVs as the political-selector case.

## 4. The morphological-pattern HIT launders the object-level MISS and OPEN

**Location:** Problem-level row: “Holds across the cases he named and the ones he could not — the pattern is doing real work, not decorating a metaphor.”

**Problem:** The pattern as stated is four parts: bad copying prototypes, radiation, gigantism-before-decline, relics in lateral branches (pp. 33–36). Gigantism is untested in 2026. The relic branch is the essay’s own OPEN and is failing from the wrong end. The successor the pattern was asked to name is the essay’s MISS. The receipts are VTOL, AM-radio (mismatch, finding 3), and EV share (the conservative substitution the essay says he failed to predict). “He got the idea” is paying for “he got the thing wrong.”

**Fix:** Split or narrow. A problem-level HIT can cover only the parts that survived contact (copying prototypes; radiation into niches), with gigantism and relics left OPEN or unscored. Do not cite EV share as evidence the pattern holds.
