## 1. **CONFIRMED (G1):** the frame says Lem priced conversation, while p. 206 prices two full phantomats

**Location:** metadata: “a channel he priced as the hard one: conversation”; lede and “What happened”: “the channel he priced as expensive”; versus “Where he was worst”: “he did not price the *channel*.”

**Verification:** On pp. 202–203 and 206, both the “travel” and “human-producing” machines are bidirectional, nerve-connected phantomats. The latter adds an autonomous interlocutor inside the synthetic world. Lem says that complete device is harder than the travel phantomat; he never compares full-sensorium simulation with a text channel. The essay’s final critical paragraph reads the passage correctly, but its title frame, lede, metadata, and transition do not.

**Fix:** Say that the essay—not Lem—transposes his detection game to a cheap text channel. Remove every formulation saying he priced conversation or the channel as expensive.

**Location and verification:**

**Fix:** Say “investigational implant”; invert the latency point (“the lunar delay remains disqualifying”); and describe 2023 as reproducible rat-kidney vitrification, storage, nanowarming, and life-sustaining transplantation—not the first mammalian organ.

A6’s blanket claim that every 2026 statement is marked and accurate is therefore **REJECTED**. Its Reality Labs aside does not validate the rest and describes the total as a projection even though the local row cites a July 2026 report.
