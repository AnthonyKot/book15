### 1. The cerebromatics `HIT` treats a scoped impossibility as a confirmed law

**Location:** Object-level row: “Cerebromatics: ‘it is impossible to add … absent traits to one’s psyche’ (p. 216)” → `HIT`, receipted by “Neural write remains stimulation and modulation; no skill or trait has been inserted.” Prose: “no skill or trait inserted.”

**Problem:** Three faults, any one of which kills the verdict.

(a) *Misreading.* The sentence is about the maneuvers just listed — lobotomy, regression, disinhibition. Two sentences later: “We will not turn Mr. Smith into Napoleon **through such maneuvers**” (p. 216). On p. 217 he treats direct write of a skill (bicycle-riding “by just introducing the appropriate information into his brain”) as a different problem, “not simple even on a theoretical level,” and then discusses a genetic route. The essay quotes the impossibility and drops the scope.

(b) *Verdict inflation.* `HIT` requires that the thing described exists or happened as described. “Has not been inserted in 2026 BCI trials” does not establish “impossible.” Absence is consistent with the claim; it does not confirm it. `OPEN` (impossibility untested) or `EARLY` (memory-prosthesis work as a dated path toward adding function) is the honest word.

(c) *Layers.* A theoretical limit is sitting in the object block and being paid by “the object hasn’t been built.” That is the mix AGENT forbids.

The absence claim is also too clean for 2026: hippocampal memory-prosthesis trials (Hampson/Berger/Roeder, human epilepsy patients) report content-specific stimulation that facilitates encoding and recall. That is not “musical talent,” but it is write-side work aimed at adding a function, and the row’s receipt does not mention it.

**Fix:** Quote “through such maneuvers.” Move the row to problem level, or split: object-level `OPEN`/`EARLY` on trait/skill insertion; problem-level `OPEN` on the impossibility. If the absence claim stays, narrow it and receipt the memory-prosthesis literature.

**Fix:** Use Guess only as a null on ranking-off; use Gauthier only as an asymmetric ranking-on result, with the nulls named. Do not say the evidence sits where his two sentences sit.
