### 1. The throne is a *reductio*, not a forecast — and the “wrong building” thesis misreads the chapter

**Location:** “He put the machine in the wrong building”; ledger MISS, “A Black Box ‘on the throne of economic power’ regulating a national economy (p. 117)”; RA/WR, “Where the box lives: the state’s economic regulator, installed by decision.”

**Problem:** Lem does not predict a state economic ruler. He introduces it with “Let us imagine” after “It might seem that dangers of this kind will be nipped in the bud by constructing a ‘black box’ of a higher type” (p. 117), then closes: “We have thus conducted a *reductio ad absurdum* of the theory of the Black Box: The Regulator of the Highest Kind” (p. 120). The prescription is “undesirable” (p. 121) and “possible yet undesirable” (p. 122). The first worked example is already a private firm — Beer’s steel mill (p. 115). Grading the throne as a failed object-level prophecy, and “wrong building” as what he got worst, scores him for a proposal he was dismantling. Cybersyn tests Beer, not a Lem forecast.

**Fix:** Drop the throne MISS and the “wrong building” frame. Score the *reductio* as problem-level (cryptocracy / don’t build rulers). If an object-level state-machine row is needed, use the coordinators passage (pp. 158–160), which is the actual forecast.

### 2. RealPage is the opposite of rival homeostats bankrupting competitors

**Location:** “his rival homeostats colluding without a meeting arrived as algorithmic pricing”; ledger HIT, “Rival homeostats in a ‘struggle for life,’ one ‘bankrupting all its competitors’… DOJ v. RealPage.”

**Problem:** Lem’s case is competing homeostats in a “struggle for life,” one “performing its set task too well, by bankrupting all its competitors,” risking “economic collapse in the country as a whole” (p. 116). RealPage is one vendor’s software that landlords feed data into; the DOJ alleged *coordination to raise rents* — a hub, not autonomous rivals destroying each other. Collusion ≠ hyper-competition. The prose even says “colluding,” which concedes the mismatch the HIT then ignores. Hedging “in the pricing form; no economy-wide collapse” does not repair a reversed mechanism.

**Fix:** Cut the HIT. Use a documented case of independent pricing agents learning to collude, or score p. 116 as MISS / RIGHT QUESTION, WRONG ANSWER. Facebook MSI already carries “task too well” in the prose; don’t spend this row on a cartel.
